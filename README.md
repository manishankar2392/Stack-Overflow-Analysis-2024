Tech Workforce Trends: Earnings, Work Modes & AI Sentiment
📊 Overview
This project analyzes global technology workforce data, focusing on three key dimensions:

Earnings & Tools – Salary distributions, programming languages, and tools used by high earners.

Remote Work & Job Satisfaction – How different work arrangements (remote, hybrid, in-person) impact satisfaction across countries.

Experience & AI Sentiment – Correlation between years of experience, AI adoption, and perceptions of AI as opportunity or threat.

🔑 Key Insights
High Earners & Tech Trends

Median salary: $65,000; 90th percentile: $167,000.

Python dominates among high earners, followed by Java and C#.

Tools like Docker, Pip, and Make are most common among top earners.

Remote Work & Job Satisfaction

Remote workers report higher satisfaction (7.1) compared to in-person (6.6).

Hybrid arrangements show balanced satisfaction but vary by country.

32% of respondents work remotely, with the U.S. showing strong satisfaction scores across modes.

Experience & AI Sentiment

AI usage is highest among professionals with 0–2 years experience (71%).

Favorable AI sentiment is strong (72%), but 12% perceive AI as a threat.

Senior professionals (16+ years) show lower AI adoption compared to juniors.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Data Source: Survey dataset (65,437 respondents)

Visualization Tools: Power BI / Tableau (depending on implementation)

Focus Areas: Salary analysis, remote work trends, AI adoption

This project is designed to:
