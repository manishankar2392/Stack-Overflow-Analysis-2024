# 📊 Stack Overflow Analysis 2024

An interactive **Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2024 Analysis Dashboard** built using **Power BI** to analyze global technology workforce trends across salary, programming languages, remote work, job satisfaction, professional experience, and AI adoption.

This project transforms survey data from **65,437 respondents** into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards and data storytelling.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2024** dataset across three major dimensions:

### 💰 Earnings & Technology Trends

* Salary distribution and percentile analysis
* Programming languages used by high earners
* Popular databases, platforms, and developer tools
* Comparison of technology preferences across professionals

### 🌍 Remote Work & Job Satisfaction

* Job satisfaction across different work arrangements
* Remote, hybrid, and in-person work comparison
* Country-wise remote work trends
* Relationship between work arrangement and satisfaction

### 🤖 Experience & AI Sentiment

* AI adoption across experience levels
* AI usage among junior and senior professionals
* Positive and negative perceptions of AI
* Relationship between professional experience and AI adoption

# 🔑 Key Insights

## 💰 High Earners & Technology Trends

* The **median salary is approximately $65,000**.
* The **90th percentile salary is approximately $167,000**.
* **Python** is highly represented among high earners, followed by **Java and C#**.
* Developer tools such as **Docker, Pip, and Make** are commonly used among top earners.
* Technology preferences vary across different salary groups and professional backgrounds.

---

## 🌍 Remote Work & Job Satisfaction

* Remote professionals report an average satisfaction score of approximately **7.1**.
* In-person professionals report approximately **6.6**.
* Hybrid work provides a balance between remote and in-person arrangements.
* Approximately **32% of respondents work remotely**.
* Remote-work patterns and satisfaction levels vary across countries.
* The **United States** shows strong satisfaction across different work arrangements.

---

## 🤖 Experience & AI Sentiment

* AI usage is highest among professionals with **0–2 years of experience**, at approximately **71%**.
* Approximately **72%** of respondents have favorable AI sentiment.
* Approximately **12%** perceive AI as a threat.
* Professionals with **16+ years of experience** show comparatively lower AI adoption.
* The analysis indicates that AI adoption is particularly strong among newer professionals.
